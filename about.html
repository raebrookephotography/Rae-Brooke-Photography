Hey, I'm Rae! (Rachael) A photographer from Raleigh, North Carolina drawn to the people, places, and moments that make life meaningful.

I believe the best photographs do more than show what a moment looked like. They bring you back to how it felt to be there.

I'm drawn to genuine connection, the little in-between moments, and the details that might otherwise go unnoticed. My goal is to create photographs that feel natural, thoughtful, and true to the people in them.

Whether we're celebrating something special or simply capturing a season of life, I want you to feel comfortable being yourself and leave with images you'll love for years to come.
