THE PERSON BEHIND THE LENS

ABOUT

Well, hello there. I'm Rae.

A photographer living in Raleigh, North Carolina, drawn to the people, places, and moments that make life meaningful.

I’m Rachael (but you can call me Rae) — a New England native, wife of Jeff, and dog mom to Wrigley. I’ve spent the past year chasing light, capturing laughter, and turning little in-between moments into memories that last forever.

I'm drawn to genuine connection, the little in-between moments, and the details that might otherwise go unnoticed. My goal is to create photographs that feel natural, thoughtful, and true to the people in them.

Whether it’s couples, four-legged friends, grads, families, or just you living your best life, I love creating images that feel like you.
